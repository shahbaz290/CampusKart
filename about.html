About

CampusKart is a campus-exclusive marketplace designed to simplify the exchange of products among college students. It provides a platform where students can buy, sell, or donate academic materials and everyday essentials within their own college community. Whether it's textbooks, lab coats, calculators, engineering drawing kits, electronics, or hostel items, CampusKart makes finding affordable and useful products quick and convenient. By connecting students directly, the platform encourages cost-effective shopping while reducing unnecessary waste through reuse and donation.

At CampusKart, our mission is to create a trusted and accessible marketplace that helps students save money, reduce waste, and make better use of available resources. College life often requires purchasing expensive study materials and equipment that may only be needed for a short period. CampusKart aims to bridge the gap between students who no longer need these items and those looking for affordable alternatives. Through this platform, we hope to encourage responsible consumption and build a stronger sense of community within educational institutions.

Why CampusKart?

Many students own textbooks, lab coats, calculators, electronics, and hostel essentials that are no longer in use. At the same time, other students need these items but may not want to purchase them at full price. CampusKart bridges this gap by connecting buyers and sellers within the same college, making transactions more convenient and trustworthy.

Future Vision

CampusKart is currently developed as a frontend prototype to demonstrate the concept of a campus marketplace. In future versions, the platform can be enhanced with user authentication, college email verification, product search and filtering, real-time chat, secure online payments, ratings and reviews, and support for multiple colleges.

CampusKart strives to build a smarter and more connected student community by making buying, selling, and donating simple, affordable, and sustainable. Through technology, the platform encourages students to make better use of available resources while helping others within their campus.
