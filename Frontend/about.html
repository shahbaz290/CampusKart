About CampusKart

Making campus life more affordable, connected and sustainable.

Who We Are

CampusKart is a student-focused marketplace that makes it easier to buy, sell and donate products within the student community. From textbooks and lab coats to calculators, engineering drawing kits, electronics and hostel essentials, CampusKart helps students find useful products at affordable prices.

Instead of letting unused items gather dust, students can list them for sale or donate them to someone who needs them. Our goal is to make exchanging everyday essentials simpler and more convenient.

Our Mission

College life comes with several expenses, and many study materials are needed only for a limited period. CampusKart aims to connect students who have unused products with those looking for affordable alternatives.

We want to encourage reuse, reduce unnecessary waste and help students save money while building a more connected student community.

What Can You Do on CampusKart?

Buy

Discover useful products listed by other students at affordable prices.

Sell

List your unused books, equipment and everyday essentials for other students.

Donate

Give your unused items a second life by making them available to someone who needs them.

Why CampusKart?

CampusKart brings buying, selling and donating together in one convenient platform. With user accounts, product listings, image uploads, search, category filters and sorting, students can easily discover and manage products.

Whether you want to find an affordable textbook, sell an old calculator or donate hostel essentials, CampusKart makes the process straightforward.

Our Future Vision

We aim to continue improving CampusKart with features such as college email verification, real-time student communication, ratings and reviews, and support for multiple colleges.

Our long-term vision is to build a trusted student marketplace that promotes affordability, accessibility and sustainable consumption.
